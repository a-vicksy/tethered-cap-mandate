the tethered cap mandate

A policy-led design intervention to eliminate plastic cap leakage by keeping every cap attached to its bottle from manufacturing to recycling.

It’s already an EU regulation. `We are now campaigning to make it one in India too. We need your help though. Just like caps need to stay attached to bottles, we need you to attach your name to the mandate - so the government makes it a national priority. Let’s together make tethered caps a design mandate for all plastic bottles in India.

And end bottle cap pollution for good.

The mandate progress so far: Aakash Ranison vs. CPCB & Ors. NGT OA No. 137/2026

Filed before NGT Principal Bench, New Delhi.

First hearing. Bench issued notice to respondents for filing their response by way of affidavit before the Tribunal at least one week before the next date of hearing through e-filing.

Second hearing. Heard jointly with the two tagged EPR cases.

Third hearing. CPCB submitted data showing only 76% compliance with the overall EPR target for rigid plastic, in a matter related to pollution from the widespread use of plastic bottle caps.

Respondent replies, filed and made public. Hindustan Coca-Cola Beverages argued the application is unsupported, cites no environmental law breaches, and seeks legislative policy changes rather than legal remedies.

PepsiCo argued the design is not required under Indian law and that the application lacks evidence that caps significantly contribute to plastic litter.

Next hearing confirmed.
