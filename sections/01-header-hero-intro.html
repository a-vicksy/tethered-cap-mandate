Attach Yourself

the tethered cap mandate

A policy-led design intervention to eliminate plastic cap leakage by keeping every cap attached to its bottle, from manufacturing to recycling.

Tethered caps are already law across all 27 countries of the European Union, with Gabon introducing a similar mandate in 2026, and Turkey and Australia moving toward one. Brands in markets like the UK, UAE, and Switzerland have adopted tethered caps voluntarily, even without a mandate in place.

Aakash Ranison has taken this to the National Green Tribunal through a Public Interest Litigation, and the Greener Earth Foundation is building the public movement behind it.

Together, we are asking the Central Pollution Control Board, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, the Bureau of Indian Standards, and the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India to make tethered caps a design requirement for every plastic bottle sold in India.

Just like caps need to stay attached to bottles, we need you to attach your name to the mandate, so this becomes a national priority.

Let’s attach ourselves and end bottle cap pollution for good.
